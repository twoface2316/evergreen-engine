Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Newport, RI?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the NEWPORT ROSE, RI US station, the last spring frost in Newport typically falls around April 7. A cautious gardener might wait until April 21 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while March 28 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Newport, RI in fall?

The first fall frost in Newport typically arrives around November 13. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as October 31; in milder years it can hold off until November 27.

How long is the growing season in Newport, RI?

Newport's average frost-free growing season is about 216 days, running from the typical last spring frost (April 7) to the typical first fall frost (November 13).

What USDA hardiness zone is Newport, RI in?

Newport, RI is in USDA plant hardiness zone 7a, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Mild winters and a long growing season, room for both cool- and warm-season crops.
