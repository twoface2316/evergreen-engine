FrostCal is an independent reference site and is not affiliated with NOAA or the USDA. See the methodology page for how the numbers are produced.

This page is for general gardening reference only and is not agronomic, professional, or safety advice. Frost dates are statistical probabilities from historical climate normals, not guarantees — always check a local forecast before planting or protecting tender plants.

Frost and temperature normals: NOAA NCEI 1991–2020 U.S. Climate Normals.

City and place data: GeoNames.org, used under CC BY 4.0.
