Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Frankford, MD?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the MD SCI CTR BALTIMORE, MD US station, the last spring frost in Frankford typically falls around March 24. A cautious gardener might wait until April 4 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while March 8 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Frankford, MD in fall?

The first fall frost in Frankford typically arrives around November 21. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as November 7; in milder years it can hold off until December 8.

How long is the growing season in Frankford, MD?

Frankford's average frost-free growing season is about 244 days, running from the typical last spring frost (March 24) to the typical first fall frost (November 21).

What USDA hardiness zone is Frankford, MD in?

Frankford, MD is in USDA plant hardiness zone 7b, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Mild winters and a long growing season, room for both cool- and warm-season crops.
