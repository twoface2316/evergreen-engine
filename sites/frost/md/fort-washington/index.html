Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Fort Washington, MD?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the OXON HILL, MD US station, the last spring frost in Fort Washington typically falls around April 3. A cautious gardener might wait until April 15 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while March 19 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Fort Washington, MD in fall?

The first fall frost in Fort Washington typically arrives around November 8. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as October 24; in milder years it can hold off until November 23.

How long is the growing season in Fort Washington, MD?

Fort Washington's average frost-free growing season is about 217 days, running from the typical last spring frost (April 3) to the typical first fall frost (November 8).

What USDA hardiness zone is Fort Washington, MD in?

Fort Washington, MD is in USDA plant hardiness zone 8a, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Mild winters and a long growing season, room for both cool- and warm-season crops.
