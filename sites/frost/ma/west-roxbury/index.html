Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in West Roxbury, MA?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the JAMAICA PLAIN, MA US station, the last spring frost in West Roxbury typically falls around April 19. A cautious gardener might wait until May 3 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while April 6 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in West Roxbury, MA in fall?

The first fall frost in West Roxbury typically arrives around October 23. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as October 8; in milder years it can hold off until November 5.

How long is the growing season in West Roxbury, MA?

West Roxbury's average frost-free growing season is about 185 days, running from the typical last spring frost (April 19) to the typical first fall frost (October 23).

What USDA hardiness zone is West Roxbury, MA in?

West Roxbury, MA is in USDA plant hardiness zone 6b, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. A classic four-season climate with cold winters and a solid summer growing season.
