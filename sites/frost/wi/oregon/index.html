Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Oregon, WI?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the UW ARBORETUM - MADISON, WI US station, the last spring frost in Oregon typically falls around May 1. A cautious gardener might wait until May 17 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while April 18 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Oregon, WI in fall?

The first fall frost in Oregon typically arrives around October 9. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as September 26; in milder years it can hold off until October 24.

How long is the growing season in Oregon, WI?

Oregon's average frost-free growing season is about 161 days, running from the typical last spring frost (May 1) to the typical first fall frost (October 9).

What USDA hardiness zone is Oregon, WI in?

Oregon, WI is in USDA plant hardiness zone 5a, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. A classic four-season climate with cold winters and a solid summer growing season.
