Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in West Town, IL?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the CHICAGO NORTHERLY IS, IL US station, the last spring frost in West Town typically falls around April 10. A cautious gardener might wait until April 25 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while March 29 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in West Town, IL in fall?

The first fall frost in West Town typically arrives around November 5. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as October 24; in milder years it can hold off until November 20.

How long is the growing season in West Town, IL?

West Town's average frost-free growing season is about 208 days, running from the typical last spring frost (April 10) to the typical first fall frost (November 5).

What USDA hardiness zone is West Town, IL in?

West Town, IL is in USDA plant hardiness zone 6a, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. A classic four-season climate with cold winters and a solid summer growing season.
