Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Evanston, WY?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the EVANSTON 1 E, WY US station, the last spring frost in Evanston typically falls around June 12. A cautious gardener might wait until July 2 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while May 26 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Evanston, WY in fall?

The first fall frost in Evanston typically arrives around September 12. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as August 26; in milder years it can hold off until September 25.

How long is the growing season in Evanston, WY?

Evanston's average frost-free growing season is about 90 days, running from the typical last spring frost (June 12) to the typical first fall frost (September 12).

What USDA hardiness zone is Evanston, WY in?

Evanston, WY is in USDA plant hardiness zone 5a, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. A classic four-season climate with cold winters and a solid summer growing season.
