Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Overland Park, KS?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the OLATHE 3E, KS US station, the last spring frost in Overland Park typically falls around April 10. A cautious gardener might wait until April 24 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while March 27 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Overland Park, KS in fall?

The first fall frost in Overland Park typically arrives around October 29. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as October 13; in milder years it can hold off until November 12.

How long is the growing season in Overland Park, KS?

Overland Park's average frost-free growing season is about 202 days, running from the typical last spring frost (April 10) to the typical first fall frost (October 29).

What USDA hardiness zone is Overland Park, KS in?

Overland Park, KS is in USDA plant hardiness zone 6b, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. A classic four-season climate with cold winters and a solid summer growing season.
