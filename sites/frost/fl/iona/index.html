Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Iona, FL?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the FT MYERS PAGE FLD AP, FL US station, the last spring frost in Iona typically falls around January 16. A cautious gardener might wait until February 2 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while January 1 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Iona, FL in fall?

The first fall frost in Iona typically arrives around January 13. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as December 31; in milder years it can hold off until February 2.

How long is the growing season in Iona, FL?

Iona's average frost-free growing season is about 365 days, running from the typical last spring frost (January 16) to the typical first fall frost (January 13).

What USDA hardiness zone is Iona, FL in?

Iona, FL is in USDA plant hardiness zone 10b, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Subtropical to frost-free — heat, not cold, is the main limit on what you can grow.
