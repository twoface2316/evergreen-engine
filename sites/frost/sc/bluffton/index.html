Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Bluffton, SC?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the BEAUFORT WWTP, SC US station, the last spring frost in Bluffton typically falls around March 3. A cautious gardener might wait until March 20 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while February 2 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Bluffton, SC in fall?

The first fall frost in Bluffton typically arrives around December 9. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as November 21; in milder years it can hold off until January 4.

How long is the growing season in Bluffton, SC?

Bluffton's average frost-free growing season is about 282 days, running from the typical last spring frost (March 3) to the typical first fall frost (December 9).

What USDA hardiness zone is Bluffton, SC in?

Bluffton, SC is in USDA plant hardiness zone 9a, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Subtropical to frost-free — heat, not cold, is the main limit on what you can grow.
