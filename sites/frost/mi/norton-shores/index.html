Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Norton Shores, MI?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the MUSKEGON CO AP, MI US station, the last spring frost in Norton Shores typically falls around April 30. A cautious gardener might wait until May 18 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while April 17 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Norton Shores, MI in fall?

The first fall frost in Norton Shores typically arrives around October 19. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as October 2; in milder years it can hold off until November 3.

How long is the growing season in Norton Shores, MI?

Norton Shores's average frost-free growing season is about 170 days, running from the typical last spring frost (April 30) to the typical first fall frost (October 19).

What USDA hardiness zone is Norton Shores, MI in?

Norton Shores, MI is in USDA plant hardiness zone 6b, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. A classic four-season climate with cold winters and a solid summer growing season.
