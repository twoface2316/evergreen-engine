Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Rochester, MI?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the PONTIAC WWTP, MI US station, the last spring frost in Rochester typically falls around April 26. A cautious gardener might wait until May 12 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while April 15 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Rochester, MI in fall?

The first fall frost in Rochester typically arrives around October 22. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as October 7; in milder years it can hold off until November 4.

How long is the growing season in Rochester, MI?

Rochester's average frost-free growing season is about 177 days, running from the typical last spring frost (April 26) to the typical first fall frost (October 22).

What USDA hardiness zone is Rochester, MI in?

Rochester, MI is in USDA plant hardiness zone 6a, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. A classic four-season climate with cold winters and a solid summer growing season.
