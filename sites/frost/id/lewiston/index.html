Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Lewiston, ID?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the LEWISTON NEZ PERCE CO AP, ID US station, the last spring frost in Lewiston typically falls around April 6. A cautious gardener might wait until April 26 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while March 20 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Lewiston, ID in fall?

The first fall frost in Lewiston typically arrives around October 26. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as October 10; in milder years it can hold off until November 12.

How long is the growing season in Lewiston, ID?

Lewiston's average frost-free growing season is about 200 days, running from the typical last spring frost (April 6) to the typical first fall frost (October 26).

What USDA hardiness zone is Lewiston, ID in?
