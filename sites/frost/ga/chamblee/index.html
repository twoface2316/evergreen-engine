Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Chamblee, GA?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the ATLANTA PEACHTREE AP, GA US station, the last spring frost in Chamblee typically falls around March 25. A cautious gardener might wait until April 12 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while March 5 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Chamblee, GA in fall?

The first fall frost in Chamblee typically arrives around November 9. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as October 26; in milder years it can hold off until November 27.

How long is the growing season in Chamblee, GA?

Chamblee's average frost-free growing season is about 228 days, running from the typical last spring frost (March 25) to the typical first fall frost (November 9).

What USDA hardiness zone is Chamblee, GA in?
