Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Anacostia, DC?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the WASHINGTON REAGAN AP, VA US station, the last spring frost in Anacostia typically falls around March 24. A cautious gardener might wait until April 6 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while March 7 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Anacostia, DC in fall?

The first fall frost in Anacostia typically arrives around November 18. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as November 5; in milder years it can hold off until December 6.

How long is the growing season in Anacostia, DC?

Anacostia's average frost-free growing season is about 241 days, running from the typical last spring frost (March 24) to the typical first fall frost (November 18).

What USDA hardiness zone is Anacostia, DC in?

Anacostia, DC is in USDA plant hardiness zone 8a, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Mild winters and a long growing season, room for both cool- and warm-season crops.
