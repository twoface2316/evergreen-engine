Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Merrydale, LA?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the BATON ROUGE RYAN AP, LA US station, the last spring frost in Merrydale typically falls around February 25. A cautious gardener might wait until March 19 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while January 29 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Merrydale, LA in fall?

The first fall frost in Merrydale typically arrives around November 26. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as November 7; in milder years it can hold off until December 19.

How long is the growing season in Merrydale, LA?

Merrydale's average frost-free growing season is about 276 days, running from the typical last spring frost (February 25) to the typical first fall frost (November 26).

What USDA hardiness zone is Merrydale, LA in?

Merrydale, LA is in USDA plant hardiness zone 9a, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Subtropical to frost-free — heat, not cold, is the main limit on what you can grow.
