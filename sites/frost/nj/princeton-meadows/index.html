Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Princeton Meadows, NJ?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the HIGHTSTOWN 2 W, NJ US station, the last spring frost in Princeton Meadows typically falls around April 25. A cautious gardener might wait until May 12 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while April 12 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Princeton Meadows, NJ in fall?

The first fall frost in Princeton Meadows typically arrives around October 16. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as October 4; in milder years it can hold off until October 29.

How long is the growing season in Princeton Meadows, NJ?

Princeton Meadows's average frost-free growing season is about 173 days, running from the typical last spring frost (April 25) to the typical first fall frost (October 16).

What USDA hardiness zone is Princeton Meadows, NJ in?

Princeton Meadows, NJ is in USDA plant hardiness zone 7a, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Mild winters and a long growing season, room for both cool- and warm-season crops.
