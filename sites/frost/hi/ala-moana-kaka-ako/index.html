Frequently Asked Questions

Does Ala Moana - Kaka'ako, HI get frost?

No. Based on NOAA's 1991–2020 climate normals for the WAIKIKI 717.2, HI US station, Ala Moana - Kaka'ako did not record a measurable freeze (32°F or below), so it's classified as frost-free.

Can I garden year-round in Ala Moana - Kaka'ako, HI?

Yes. Without a hard frost limiting the season, most crops can be planted nearly any time of year in Ala Moana - Kaka'ako. Timing shifts around summer heat and rainy/dry seasons rather than frost risk.

What's the typical temperature range in Ala Moana - Kaka'ako, HI?

Average monthly lows range from about 66°F to 75°F, and average monthly highs range from about 81°F to 88°F, based on 1991–2020 NOAA normals.

What USDA hardiness zone is Ala Moana - Kaka'ako, HI in?

Ala Moana - Kaka'ako, HI is in USDA plant hardiness zone 12b, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Tropical, essentially frost-free conditions year-round.
