Frequently Asked Questions

Does Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI get frost?

No. Based on NOAA's 1991–2020 climate normals for the HILO 5 S, HI US station, Hawaiian Paradise Park did not record a measurable freeze (32°F or below), so it's classified as frost-free.

Can I garden year-round in Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI?

Yes. Without a hard frost limiting the season, most crops can be planted nearly any time of year in Hawaiian Paradise Park. Timing shifts around summer heat and rainy/dry seasons rather than frost risk.

What's the typical temperature range in Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI?

Average monthly lows range from about 61°F to 67°F, and average monthly highs range from about 75°F to 80°F, based on 1991–2020 NOAA normals.

What USDA hardiness zone is Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI in?

Hawaiian Paradise Park, HI is in USDA plant hardiness zone 12b, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Tropical, essentially frost-free conditions year-round.
