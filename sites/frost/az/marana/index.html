Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Marana, AZ?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the TUCSON 17 NW, AZ US station, the last spring frost in Marana typically falls around February 23. A cautious gardener might wait until March 22 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while January 15 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Marana, AZ in fall?

The first fall frost in Marana typically arrives around December 6. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as November 18; in milder years it can hold off until December 25.

How long is the growing season in Marana, AZ?

Marana's average frost-free growing season is about 287 days, running from the typical last spring frost (February 23) to the typical first fall frost (December 6).

What USDA hardiness zone is Marana, AZ in?

Marana, AZ is in USDA plant hardiness zone 9b, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Subtropical to frost-free — heat, not cold, is the main limit on what you can grow.
