Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in West Saint Paul, MN?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the ST PAUL DOWNTOWN AP, MN US station, the last spring frost in West Saint Paul typically falls around April 24. A cautious gardener might wait until May 10 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while April 9 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in West Saint Paul, MN in fall?

The first fall frost in West Saint Paul typically arrives around October 16. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as October 2; in milder years it can hold off until October 29.

How long is the growing season in West Saint Paul, MN?

West Saint Paul's average frost-free growing season is about 174 days, running from the typical last spring frost (April 24) to the typical first fall frost (October 16).

What USDA hardiness zone is West Saint Paul, MN in?

West Saint Paul, MN is in USDA plant hardiness zone 5a, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. A classic four-season climate with cold winters and a solid summer growing season.
