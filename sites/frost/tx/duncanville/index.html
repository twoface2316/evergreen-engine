Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Duncanville, TX?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the DALLAS REDBIRD AP, TX US station, the last spring frost in Duncanville typically falls around March 6. A cautious gardener might wait until March 26 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while February 15 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Duncanville, TX in fall?

The first fall frost in Duncanville typically arrives around November 25. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as November 6; in milder years it can hold off until December 14.

How long is the growing season in Duncanville, TX?

Duncanville's average frost-free growing season is about 265 days, running from the typical last spring frost (March 6) to the typical first fall frost (November 25).

What USDA hardiness zone is Duncanville, TX in?

Duncanville, TX is in USDA plant hardiness zone 8b, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Mild winters and a long growing season, room for both cool- and warm-season crops.
