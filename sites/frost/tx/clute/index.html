Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Clute, TX?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the FREEPORT 2 NW, TX US station, the last spring frost in Clute typically falls around January 27. A cautious gardener might wait until February 28 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while December 24 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Clute, TX in fall?

The first fall frost in Clute typically arrives around January 1. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as December 1; in milder years it can hold off until January 30.

How long is the growing season in Clute, TX?

Clute's average frost-free growing season is about 338 days, running from the typical last spring frost (January 27) to the typical first fall frost (January 1).

What USDA hardiness zone is Clute, TX in?

Clute, TX is in USDA plant hardiness zone 9b, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Subtropical to frost-free — heat, not cold, is the main limit on what you can grow.
