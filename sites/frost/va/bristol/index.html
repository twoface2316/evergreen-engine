Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Bristol, VA?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the ABINGDON 3S, VA US station, the last spring frost in Bristol typically falls around April 21. A cautious gardener might wait until May 10 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while April 5 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Bristol, VA in fall?

The first fall frost in Bristol typically arrives around October 20. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as October 8; in milder years it can hold off until November 3.

How long is the growing season in Bristol, VA?

Bristol's average frost-free growing season is about 182 days, running from the typical last spring frost (April 21) to the typical first fall frost (October 20).

What USDA hardiness zone is Bristol, VA in?

Bristol, VA is in USDA plant hardiness zone 7a, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Mild winters and a long growing season, room for both cool- and warm-season crops.
