Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in San Juan Capistrano, CA?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the LAGUNA BEACH, CA US station, the last spring frost in San Juan Capistrano typically falls around January 6. A cautious gardener might wait until February 11 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while December 13 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in San Juan Capistrano, CA in fall?

The first fall frost in San Juan Capistrano typically arrives around January 2. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as December 12; in milder years it can hold off until February 8.

How long is the growing season in San Juan Capistrano, CA?

San Juan Capistrano's average frost-free growing season is about 365 days, running from the typical last spring frost (January 6) to the typical first fall frost (January 2).

What USDA hardiness zone is San Juan Capistrano, CA in?
