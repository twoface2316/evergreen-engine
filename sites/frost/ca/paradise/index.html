Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Paradise, CA?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the PARADISE, CA US station, the last spring frost in Paradise typically falls around February 7. A cautious gardener might wait until April 1 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while December 27 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Paradise, CA in fall?

The first fall frost in Paradise typically arrives around December 18. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as November 26; in milder years it can hold off until January 27.

How long is the growing season in Paradise, CA?

Paradise's average frost-free growing season is about 313 days, running from the typical last spring frost (February 7) to the typical first fall frost (December 18).

What USDA hardiness zone is Paradise, CA in?

Paradise, CA is in USDA plant hardiness zone 9b, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Subtropical to frost-free — heat, not cold, is the main limit on what you can grow.
