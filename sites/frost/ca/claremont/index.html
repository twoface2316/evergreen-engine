Frequently Asked Questions

When is the last frost in Claremont, CA?

Based on 1991–2020 NOAA climate normals for the POMONA/FAIRPLEX, CA US station, the last spring frost in Claremont typically falls around January 8. A cautious gardener might wait until February 18 (only a 10% chance of a later frost), while December 14 marks an earlier, higher-risk planting date.

When is the first frost in Claremont, CA in fall?

The first fall frost in Claremont typically arrives around December 28. To stay safe, assume frost could start as early as December 5; in milder years it can hold off until February 2.

How long is the growing season in Claremont, CA?

Claremont's average frost-free growing season is about 354 days, running from the typical last spring frost (January 8) to the typical first fall frost (December 28).

What USDA hardiness zone is Claremont, CA in?

Claremont, CA is in USDA plant hardiness zone 10a, per the USDA's 2023 Plant Hardiness Zone Map. Subtropical to frost-free — heat, not cold, is the main limit on what you can grow.
